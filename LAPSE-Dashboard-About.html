Welcome to the LAPSE Dashboard

The Legislation Applicable to Pacific Salmon and Ecosystems (LAPSE) Dashboard allows you to explore Canadian federal and British Columbia provincial legislation relevant to Pacific salmon conservation and management. This tool bridges the gap between legal language and natural resource management by mapping legislation to internationally recognized biodiversity threat categories (IUCN threats) through a novel system of management domains which is a framework which links legislation to IUCN threats.

This dashboard allows you to filter and explore legislation by management domain, jurisdiction, and specific acts or regulations—making it easier to understand the regulatory landscape governing Pacific salmon.

To learn more about the creation of management domains and the legislative review process, please refer to the LAPSE Technical Brief linked below.

How to Use the LAPSE Dashboard

Getting Started

  1. Select a Management Domain - On the left sidebar, click on a management domain (e.g., Fisheries, Pollution, Water Use) to filter legislation by that topic. Click "All" to reset.
  2. Filter by Jurisdiction - Use the toggle buttons to view Federal, Provincial, or All legislation.
  3. Search for Keywords - Enter a word or phrase in the search box to find specific content across all legislation. Use the backspace button to clear your search.
  4. Select Legislation - Choose a specific Act from the dropdown menu, then optionally select a Regulation under that Act.
  5. Explore Sections - Click on any section to expand it and read the full legislative text. Keywords will be highlighted for easy reference. Keywords highlighted in red are associated with IUCN Threats, while keywords highlighted in blue are associated with Clause Types.

Key Features

  • Management Domains: 14 categories representing administrative responsibilities for Pacific salmon (e.g., Aquaculture, Climate Change, Fisheries, Forest and Range, Mining and Energy, Spatial Designation, etc.)
  • Dual Jurisdiction View: Toggle between Federal and Provincial legislation, or view both simultaneously
  • Keyword Search: Full-text search across all collected legislation with highlighted matches
  • Hierarchical Navigation: Browse by Act, then drill down to specific Regulations
  • Interactive Visualizations: View keyword frequency, IUCN threat co-occurrence, and clause type distribution for your selected filters
  • Collapsible Sections: Expand only the sections you want to read for a cleaner viewing experience
  • Direct Links: Click "View Full Legislation" to access the complete official text on government websites
Tip: Start with a Management Domain that interests you, then use the keyword search to find specific topics. The visualizations on the right will update dynamically to show you the distribution of content related to your selection.

Understanding the Visualizations

  • Keyword Frequency: Shows the top 10 most common keywords in your filtered results, helping you understand the main themes
  • IUCN Threat Co-occurrence: Displays which IUCN threat categories appear in your selected management domain or legislation
  • Clause Type Distribution: Shows the relationship between management domains and clause types (e.g., prohibitions, definitions, penalties)

Scope Classification

Legislation in LAPSE is classified by how directly it applies to Pacific salmon:

  • 1 - Salmon: Explicitly mentions Pacific salmon species (Chinook, Sockeye, etc.)
  • 2 - Fish: Refers to fish that include salmon
  • 3 - Habitat: Refers to habitat that includes salmon habitat
  • 4 - Governance: Administrative processes and structures
